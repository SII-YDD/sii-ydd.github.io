# Di Yang — Visual direction

- Current preference: a clear academic homepage with a few friendly emoji accents and a compact personal-interest note.
- Palette: white `#ffffff`, dark gray `#25282d`, secondary gray `#555b65`, blue links `#285d91`, light separators `#e4e6e9`.
- Typography: system sans-serif; 42 px name, 22 px section headings, 16 px body. Paper title and author list have distinct hierarchy.
- Layout: a centered 880 px column, a compact navigation row, profile, news, and one selected publication. Years align in a narrow left column on desktop.
- Mobile: a single column with stacked year labels; navigation wraps below the name and utilities.
- Personal touches: a small greeting emoji, a soft gray line for basketball, hiking and long-distance running, and a medal with a pale gold award label. Decorative emoji are hidden from screen readers; text carries the meaning.
- No canvas, animated decorations, research artwork, cards or citation metrics.
- Accessibility: semantic sections, visible keyboard focus, skip link, native citation dialog, sufficient text contrast, dark mode and print styles. All core content is in static HTML.
- Template reuse: Scholar Pages bibliography and citation engine; custom presentation.
